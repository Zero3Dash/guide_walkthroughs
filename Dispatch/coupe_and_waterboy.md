# Dispatch Perfect Run Guide: Coupé and Waterboy

*Compiled 15 November 2026 from the Into Indie Games guides and the Dispatch Wiki. Sources are listed at the end.*

## Read this first

- **What “perfect” means here:** every dispatch call succeeds. This guide covers dispatching and stat upgrades only, not dialogue choices or romance.
- **Your route:** at the end of Episode 3 you cut Sonar (so Coupé stays), and in Episode 4 you recruit Waterboy over Phenomaman.
- **How much is tested:** the only full perfect-run guide I found is built on the opposite cut (Coupé cut, Sonar kept), covers only Episodes 1 to 5, and its authors mark it unfinished. Episodes 1 to 3 are the same for both routes until the cut, so those are as tested as the sources get. Episodes 4 and 5 are that guide adapted to your route by me, with picks re-screened against the stat sums below. Anything marked ⚠ is a call where no team on the projected roster clearly clears the listed stats, or where the sources don’t say what a stat check reads. Episodes 6 to 8 are stat-based estimates with no tested route behind them.
- **When the sources disagree** I followed the Perfect Run guide, because its picks are sequenced around hero cooldowns. Mixing picks from two different sequences can leave a hero resting when you need them.
- **Reading the numbers:** stat strings are (Combat, Vigor, Mobility, Charisma, Intellect). Percentages are the guides’ own figures.
- **Cooldowns:** heroes need to rest after a call. The tested order assumes you wait for the hero listed instead of substituting.

## Your two heroes

**Coupé** starts at Combat 4, Vigor 1, Mobility 3, Charisma 1, Intellect 3.
- *En Pointe:* in calls with two or more slots she gets +1 Combat in slot 1 or +1 Mobility in slot 2.
- *Synergy partner:* Punch Up. Synergy pairs do not sabotage each other.
- *Hero Training:* Pirouette retries a failed call; À la Seconde gives +3 Combat in slot 1 or +3 Mobility in slot 2. My read is that À la Seconde suits a perfect run better, since I haven’t seen it confirmed that a retried failure still counts as clean.
- *Upgrade idea (mine, untested):* En Pointe already lifts her Combat and Mobility, so her points go to Intellect, which the Intellect 10 calls in Episodes 6 to 8 need.

**Waterboy** starts at Combat 1, Vigor 2, Mobility 2, Charisma 1, Intellect 2, the weakest spread on the roster.
- *Eager Sponge:* he assigns himself to calls when he hasn’t been sent for a while, with +1 to his highest stat for that call.
- *Hero Training:* Eager Super Sponge makes that bonus +3. Holy Water Spit stops the self-assigning and heals up to two heroes.
- *Synergy partner:* Malevola, because Sonar is cut. (Punch Up is his partner only when Coupé is cut.)
- *Wiki build advice:* favour Vigor, leave Combat and Charisma low, and pair him with strong partners.

**Synergy pairs on your route:** Flambae + Prism, Invisigal + Golem, Punch Up + Coupé, Waterboy + Malevola.

## How the estimates work

Episodes 4 to 8 are picked by adding up team stats against each call’s requirements, because no tested route exists for them. Episodes 1 to 3 are unchanged.

- **The sheet:** base stats come from the Dispatch Wiki (Coupé, Waterboy) and fan guides (the rest, which disagree in places; Golem’s are the least certain). On top of that I add the upgrade points this guide hands out.
- **The check:** I add up a team’s stats and compare them with the call’s requirements in the Missions guide. “Clears” means every listed stat is met. “Short by N” means the team is N under.
- **What a gap costs:** the Perfect Run guide reports about 85% for Golem on Moving Away, where he is 2 Mobility and 1 Intellect short on my sheet, and about 63% on Mall Looting, where he is 3 Vigor and 4 Mobility short. Small gaps survive and big ones don’t. Prism copies the hero on her left at half stats and Flambae stacks bonuses, so teams with either do better than the sums say.
- **Use it as a shortlist.** The percentage the game shows when you pick a team is the final check. If it is under about 90%, try the next candidate.
- **Thresholds:** the Missions guide’s “Double XP” lines are a bonus and its “auto-fail” lines are a floor, not part of the base requirement. I only chase double XP once the base is safe.
- **Stat cap:** the wiki says Invisigal’s stats start at 11 and cap at 20. I assume the same cap for everyone, which is why Punch Up and Waterboy have the most room to grow.

## Where to bank points in Episodes 6 to 8

1. **Mobility on Waterboy, Coupé and Flambae.** Only Invisigal reaches 5, and she is in most of the Mobility 8 to 10 teams above. Spreading Mobility is what lets her rest.
2. **One Vigor point on Golem or Invisigal** for Farmers Market Attack, and one on Waterboy or Invisigal for Rescue Flambae.
3. **One Mobility point on Prism** for Help Brainbook.
4. **Combat on Flambae and Coupé** if you want a real attempt at Roast of Mayor Yao.
5. **One more Intellect on Coupé** so Reprogram the Bots and Sewer Bomb 2 stop clearing by exactly zero.
6. **Punch Up:** the tested plan never spends a point on him and he has the most room (12 of 20 on my sheet). Vigor first, since the Missions guide wants him at 5 for Fight in the Tournament.

Invisigal is the bottleneck for most Mobility calls, and Golem for most Vigor and Intellect calls. Rest them between calls and send the alternates listed when they are down.

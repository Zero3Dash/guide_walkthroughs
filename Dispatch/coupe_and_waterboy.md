# Dispatch Perfect Run Guide: Coupé and Waterboy

*Compiled 15 November 2026 from the Into Indie Games guides and the Dispatch Wiki. Sources are listed at the end.*

## Read this first

- **What “perfect” means here:** every dispatch call succeeds. This guide covers dispatching and stat upgrades only, not dialogue choices or romance.
- **Your route:** at the end of Episode 3 you cut Sonar (so Coupé stays), and in Episode 4 you recruit Waterboy over Phenomaman.
- **How much is tested:** the only full perfect-run guide I found is built on the opposite cut (Coupé cut, Sonar kept), covers only Episodes 1 to 5, and its authors mark it unfinished. Episodes 1 to 3 are the same for both routes until the cut, so those are as tested as the sources get. Episodes 4 and 5 are that guide adapted to your route by me. Anything marked ⚠ is a call where the tested pick used Sonar and I have no tested replacement. For Episodes 6 to 8 I found no tested route, only the stat gates.
- **When the sources disagree** I followed the Perfect Run guide, because its picks are sequenced around hero cooldowns. Mixing picks from two different sequences can leave a hero resting when you need them.
- **Reading the numbers:** stat strings are (Combat, Vigor, Mobility, Charisma, Intellect). Percentages are the guides’ own figures.
- **Cooldowns:** heroes need to rest after a call. The tested order assumes you wait for the hero listed instead of substituting.

## Your two heroes

**Coupé** starts at Combat 4, Vigor 1, Mobility 3, Charisma 1, Intellect 3.
- *En Pointe:* in calls with two or more slots she gets +1 Combat in slot 1 or +1 Mobility in slot 2.
- *Synergy partner:* Punch Up. Synergy pairs do not sabotage each other.
- *Hero Training:* Pirouette retries a failed call; À la Seconde gives +3 Combat in slot 1 or +3 Mobility in slot 2. My read is that À la Seconde suits a perfect run better, since I haven’t seen it confirmed that a retried failure still counts as clean.
- *Upgrade idea (mine, untested):* En Pointe already lifts her Combat and Mobility, so her real gaps are Vigor and Intellect.

**Waterboy** starts at Combat 1, Vigor 2, Mobility 2, Charisma 1, Intellect 2, the weakest spread on the roster.
- *Eager Sponge:* he assigns himself to calls when he hasn’t been sent for a while, with +1 to his highest stat for that call.
- *Hero Training:* Eager Super Sponge makes that bonus +3. Holy Water Spit stops the self-assigning and heals up to two heroes.
- *Synergy partner:* Malevola, because Sonar is cut. (Punch Up is his partner only when Coupé is cut.)
- *Wiki build advice:* favour Vigor, leave Combat and Charisma low, and pair him with strong partners.

**Synergy pairs on your route:** Flambae + Prism, Invisigal + Golem, Punch Up + Coupé, Waterboy + Malevola.

## Episode 3: Turnover

The team starts sabotaging each other. Sabotaged heroes earn no XP and can be injured. Synergy pairs don’t sabotage each other.

**Upgrade points before the shift (tested plan):**
- Sonar +1 Combat, +1 Vigor
- Invisigal +1 Charisma, +1 Intellect
- Prism +1 Vigor
- Malevola +1 Combat
- Golem +1 Charisma
- Flambae +1 Vigor

**My one addition:** Coupé +1 Mobility before the Landslide call below. Sonar’s points look wasted because he leaves at the end of this episode, but the tested plan needs them to clear his calls, and no source tests a Sonar-light Episode 3.

**Sabotage events:** pick the option whose stat your hero meets.
- Prism doxxed: “Distract them with a song” (Charisma 4), “Blast them with light” (Combat 5), “Push through the crowd” (Vigor 3)
- Golem pushed into wet concrete: “Ask someone for help” (Charisma), “Incorporate it into your body” (Intellect), “Pry yourself loose” (Vigor 4)
- Malevola led to a cult: “Fight your way out” (Combat 4), “Run” (Mobility 3), “Play the part” (Charisma 4)
- Sonar deafened by Prism: “Track down the source” (Mobility 1), “Plug up your ears” (Intellect), “Withstand the noise” (Vigor)
- **Coupé pushed into a pool** (during Landslide): “Splash” (5), “Backstroke” (Mobility 4), “Describe” (2). Choose Backstroke. Coupé’s base Mobility is 3 and En Pointe doesn’t apply to a one-slot call, hence the extra point. She is injured afterwards.

## Episodes 6 to 8

I found no tested perfect-run route for these. The Missions guide’s Episode 6 and 7 recommendations are written for a run that keeps Sonar, so they don’t apply to here.

**Stat gates worth planning around** (from the Missions guide):
- Reprogram the Bots (Ep 6): Combat below 4 auto-fails, and it needs Intellect 10
- Farmers Market Attack (Ep 7): Vigor below 8 auto-fails
- Defeat Mister Fister (Ep 8): Vigor below 7 auto-fails, and it needs Combat 10 and Mobility 10
- Defeat SmileMore (Ep 8): Charisma below 7 auto-fails. The wiki says Waterboy shouldn’t be a Charisma build, so pair him with a high-Charisma hero here.
- Ep 8 “Attack Coupé” calls belong to the storyline where Coupé is cut. On your route the wiki says Coupé is captured by the Red Ring and Sonar joins them, so expect different calls from the Missions guide’s list.

**Upgrade direction:** Waterboy toward Vigor and Mobility (the wiki’s build and the tested plan agree). Coupé toward Vigor, then Intellect. Train Coupé with À la Seconde when a Hero Training slot opens.
